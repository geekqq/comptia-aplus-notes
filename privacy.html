Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Welcome to ExamHi ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible from https://examhi.com/. We are dedicated to providing high-quality, free practice materials for IT certification candidates while respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect and how we use, disclose, and protect it.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

ExamHi is designed as a barrier-free, privacy-conscious educational tool:

  • No Required User Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal credentials (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to take our practice tests.
  • Local Exam Progress Storage: Your exam scores, answered questions, and starred bookmarks (⭐) are stored exclusively on your device using your browser's local storage (LocalStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Information Collected Automatically (Log Files)

Like most websites, ExamHi utilizes standard log files provided by our global content delivery network (Cloudflare). Information gathered automatically includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized where applicable)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Referring / exit pages and date/time stamps
  • HTTP status codes and data transferred

This information is used strictly to analyze technical performance, administer the site, prevent malicious bot attacks, and maintain server reliability.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Important Notice Regarding Advertising: ExamHi may display third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense to fund our free educational platform.

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads to our site visitors based on their visit to ExamHi and other sites on the Internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first web telemetry service that does not use client-side cookies or collect personal identifying information (PII). It measures aggregate visits without tracking individual user behavior across domains.

5. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights regarding your data:

  • The right to access, update, or delete information we possess about you.
  • The right to restrict or object to our processing of your data.
  • The right to data portability.

Because ExamHi does not retain identifiable user profiles or database accounts, most personal rights are exercisable directly by clearing your browser's cache and local storage.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Compliance

Under the CCPA / CPRA, California consumers have the right to request disclosure of personal data collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

We do not sell your personal information. Any data processed for targeted advertising can be managed using the opt-out mechanisms outlined in Section 3.

7. Children's Privacy

ExamHi does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

8. External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as certification vendors or informational resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party domains.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date."

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our team via our official GitHub repository or by email at: support@examhi.com.
